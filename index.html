About the Kochi Metro Blue Line Tracker

Track every Kochi Metro train on the Blue Line from Aluva to Thrippunithura in real time. Positions are calculated every two seconds from the official KMRL timetable in Indian Standard Time, so you can see which train is closest to your station and when it should arrive. It works in any browser, with no app or login needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common passenger and technical questions about Kochi Metro tracking.

Is this the official Kochi Metro app?
No. This is an independent tracker built by Syam Suresh, a web developer in Kochi. For official announcements, use the KMRL website or app.
Are the train positions exact?
They’re estimated from the official timetable, not GPS. If a train is delayed, the tracker won’t show it.
How many stations are on the Kochi Metro Blue Line?
25 stations across about 28 km, from Aluva in the north to Thrippunithura in the south.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. It runs in any phone browser at 60 FPS, with no download needed.
